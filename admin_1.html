Quick photo upload

Add 1–10 photos straight to a listing — no CSV needed. Photos are auto-resized to a consistent, clean size for the shop (aspect ratio always preserved, never stretched or squished).

1 · Upload CSV (for bulk imports — optional if you're just adding photos above)

Required columns: SKU, Brand, Product Name, Category, Sizes, Quantity, Condition. Optional: Fit, Description, Status, Images. Category can list more than one, separated by commas (e.g. "Hoodies, Streetwear") — each shows as its own filter on the shop page. Sizes separated by / or , — Images is a semicolon-separated list of photo filenames to match.

2 · Upload photos

Name files starting with the SKU (e.g. 4D-1001-front.jpg) and they'll auto-match, or reference exact filenames in the CSV's Images column. Every photo is auto-resized and re-encoded to a consistent, clean size on publish — same as Quick Upload.
