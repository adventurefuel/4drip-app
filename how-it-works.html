The Process

How it works

Same process, every order. Here's exactly what happens from the moment you pay to the moment it's at your door.

Step 01
You pay
Confirm your order and pay through CashApp or Venmo — whichever's easier for you.
Step 02
Order is approved
We double-check everything's correct, then confirm your order is locked in.
Step 03
Production starts
Your piece goes into production — this is where the real work begins.
Step 04
Photos sent to you
~48 hours
We send real photos of your actual product before it ships, so you know exactly what's coming.
Step 05
Tracking sent
Once your product is completed, we ship it and send you the tracking number right away.
Step 06
Delivered to your door
~10 business days
Your order arrives. That's it — no surprises.
Step 07
You order again
Ready for the next piece? Same process, every time.
Then it starts over
You pay, we approve, production starts, photos come in around 48 hours, tracking follows, and it's at your door in about 10 business days. Same process, every single order.

Times listed above are typical, not guaranteed — we'll keep you posted at every step, so you shouldn't have to ask where things stand.
